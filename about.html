About

Why this exists, how it is made, and what it rests on.

Why

Depression affects more than 300 million people. Most of them will never read a research paper about what helps, and when you are depressed you cannot concentrate on one anyway. Light for Every Day takes a library of research on depression and turns it into one short, kind, concrete message a day, in the four-part shape used by daily devotionals: a quote, an explanation, a challenge, questions for reflection.

It grew out of a conversation at the Inter-European Division of the Seventh-day Adventist Church about sharing small daily messages, by e-mail or WhatsApp, with people who are struggling. It is a sister project of Grace for Every Day.

How the messages are made

  1. A curated library of more than two hundred documents (peer-reviewed studies, WHO reports, clinical self-help manuals) was gathered by health professionals in the church.
  2. Each document was read and summarised; its key findings were tied to one of twelve themes.
  3. Each day's message was drafted from those findings with the help of AI writing tools, under strict rules: no invented quotes, no invented numbers, no medical advice beyond the evidence, never a word against professional treatment.
  4. Every message is read by a person before it is published. Readers can mark a message helpful or not, and that feedback shapes revisions.
  5. Every message lists the studies it draws on. You can open them below.

The studies are copyrighted by their publishers; we link to them rather than copy them. The CBT self-help modules "Back from the Bluez" are published free of charge by the Centre for Clinical Interventions, Western Australia, at cci.health.wa.gov.au.

What this is not

Not therapy, not a diagnosis, not a substitute for a doctor. If you have been low for more than two weeks, please see one. Everything you write in the app stays on your device; nothing you type is sent to us.

Contact

Corrections, wrong crisis numbers, ideas, or a request to review the messages in your language: write to hello@graceforevery.day.
