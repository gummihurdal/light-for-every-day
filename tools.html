How Heavy Has It Been?

This is the PHQ-9, the nine-question screening tool doctors use worldwide. It is not a diagnosis. It helps you see where you are and gives you something concrete to show a doctor. Over the last two weeks, how often have you been bothered by any of the following?

PHQ-9 © Pfizer Inc., free to use without permission (Kroenke, Spitzer & Williams, 2001). Bands: 0–4 minimal, 5–9 mild, 10–14 moderate, 15–19 moderately severe, 20–27 severe.

Slow Breathing

Four seconds in, six seconds out. Longer out-breaths calm the body's alarm system. Try one minute. Nothing to achieve; follow the circle.

Three Good Things

Each evening, write three things that went well today and, if you can, why. They can be tiny: the coffee was warm, someone held a door. In Seligman's trials this simple exercise still lifted mood six months later. On a very dark day, one thing is enough.
