Light for Every Day

Eighty-four short daily messages for people living with depression. Written with care, grounded in research, and never more than a few minutes to read.

Start day 1 Get it by e-mail

Every day has four parts

A quote
From Scripture, from the research, or from someone who has walked this road.
An explanation
What the science actually found, in plain words. Each message draws on studies from a library of over two hundred papers.
One small challenge
Something doable today, even with very little energy. Always with a smaller version if today is hard.
Questions for reflection
Two or three gentle questions. Write your answers in a private journal that never leaves your phone.

Twelve weeks, twelve doors

The journey walks through the areas where research shows real, measurable differences for people with depression. You can go in order or open any door.

This is not therapy, and it is not meant to be

Depression is a real illness and it is treatable. These messages walk beside you; they do not replace a doctor, a therapist or medication. If you have been low for more than two weeks, please talk to a doctor. If you are in crisis, use the numbers at the bottom of every page.

Who made this

Light for Every Day is a project of the same team behind Grace for Every Day, with the Inter-European Division of the Seventh-day Adventist Church. The content was drafted from a curated library of research on depression and is reviewed by people before it reaches you. Read about the method and browse the evidence library
