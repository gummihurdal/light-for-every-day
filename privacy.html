Privacy

Mental health is private. This app is built so that we learn as little about you as possible.

What stays on your device

Your start date, which challenges you marked done, your journal answers, mood check-ins, self-check scores and "three good things" are stored only in your browser (local storage). They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes them. You can export or erase them under Settings.

What reaches our servers

When you open a page, the app downloads the day's message, the list of themes and the source list from our database (hosted by Supabase in the EU). Those requests carry your IP address like any web request; we do not store it with any profile. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers also see ordinary request logs. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

If you tap "Was this helpful?", we store the answer (yes/no), the message number and the time. Nothing identifies you.

If you subscribe by e-mail

We store your e-mail address, chosen time, time zone, start date and the dates on which messages were sent, so that the series arrives in order. Your address is used only to send you the daily message and is deleted when you unsubscribe. We never share or sell it. E-mails are sent through a transactional e-mail provider acting on our behalf.

No tracking

No analytics scripts, no advertising, no cookies beyond what the browser needs to store your own data.
